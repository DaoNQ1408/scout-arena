# Design System Document

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system.

## Brand Identity

### Color Palette
Our brand uses a dark color mode.
- **Primary Color:** `#E10600` - A bold and distinctive red, used for key interactive elements and brand highlights.
- **Secondary Color:** `#6C727D` - A versatile supporting grey, ideal for less prominent UI components and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary Color:** `#FFFFFF` - A clean white accent, providing contrast and highlights.
- **Neutral Color:** `#1A1A1D` - A deep, dark background color, forming the base for our surfaces.

### Typography
The typography is designed for clarity and impact across all elements.
- **Headlines:** `spaceGrotesk` - Provides a modern and impactful feel for titles and headings.
- **Body Text:** `inter` - Chosen for its readability and versatility in longer content blocks.
- **Labels:** `spaceGrotesk` - Ensures consistency and legibility for interactive labels and small text elements.

### Shape and Form
- **Roundedness:** Our UI elements feature a subtle roundedness (level 1), providing a soft yet defined aesthetic.

### Spacing
- **Whitespace:** A normal level of spacing (level 2) is applied throughout the UI, balancing information density with visual comfort and legibility. This allows elements sufficient breathing room without feeling too sparse.
